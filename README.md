# The Twelfth Boat

An entry for make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel curious" competition.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Every morning the keeper counts the boats out of the harbour: eleven.
> Every evening: twelve. No one has seen her leave. He no longer asks where
> she comes from. He leaves her a lamp and a cup of tea, in case whoever
> sails her is cold. In the morning, the cup is always empty.

The piece is a small, calm mystery built as one curiosity loop. A keeper
counts eleven boats out of the harbour each morning and twelve back each
evening; the twelfth boat has never been seen to leave, and where she comes
from is never explained. The keeper's answer is not fear but kindness — a
lamp, and a cup of tea for an unknown sailor — and the last line quietly
recontextualizes everything before it: every morning the cup is empty, so
something out there accepts what he leaves. No question the text raises is
answered, and the final image is meant to keep working after the text ends.

The program is deterministic, needs no network, and checks before writing
that the text is within the competition's 1–280 code-point limit (the text
is 274 code points).
